Agent & Graph

I’m a Ph.D. candidate at Renmin University of China and a member of the ALGO Lab. My advisor is Prof. Zhewei Wei.

I earned my bachelor’s degree at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics. My earlier research explored multi-agent systems and robust graph learning.

I study agent harnesses

  • Long-horizon work. Help agents pick up a task where they left off.
  • Self-evolution. Help agents revise their strategies through experience.
  • Human-centered design. Make an agent’s work easy for people to understand and steer.
